Why this course exists

📊

170M new jobs by 2030

The WEF projects 170 million new jobs and 92 million displaced jobs. The net balance is positive, but reskilling isn’t keeping pace.

🇧🇷

Brazil: 18 robots / 10k workers

Global average: 151. Korea: 1,012. Brazil's robotization is growing 52% per year, but without a systemic reskilling program.

⚠️

70% of transformations fail

Not because of technology. Because of the human factor: resistance, fear, inadequate leadership preparation, and the wrong metrics.

🔐

ISO 10218:2025 changed everything

The biggest revision in 14 years. “Cobots” no longer exist as a category. Collaboration is now an application. New validation, certification, and cybersecurity requirements.

The Central Thesis

"Traditional training loses value when technology becomes autonomous. But training for human adaptation in autonomous environments gains value."

The question is how to move from “training people to perform tasks” to “training people to coexist, supervise, and make decisions in automated systems”.
